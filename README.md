# CRM Sales Opportunities: Analyzing B2B Sales Pipeline Data

## 📄 Project Description
This project analyzes B2B sales pipeline data for a fictitious company specializing in computer hardware sales. The dataset includes information about company accounts, products, sales teams, and sales opportunities. The focus is on generating actionable insights to optimize sales strategies, understand market trends, and evaluate team performance.

Through this project, I applied SQL techniques to answer critical business questions and showcase advanced querying skills.

## 🚀 Features
- **Sales Pipeline Analysis**: Track deal progress through various stages (Prospecting > Engaging > Won/Lost).
- **Revenue Insights**:
  - Total revenue by sector, region, and product series.
  - Lost revenue analysis to identify missed opportunities.
- **Performance Metrics**:
  - Sales team and agent performance evaluation.
  - Analysis of deal closure times and patterns.
- **Account Insights**:
  - Revenue contribution by major accounts.
  - Segmentation by industry, employee size, and annual revenue.

## 📊 Dataset Overview
The project uses four interconnected tables:

### **1. `accounts`**
Contains details about client companies:
| Field             | Description                          |
|--------------------|--------------------------------------|
| `account`          | Company name                        |
| `sector`           | Industry                            |
| `year_established` | Year established                    |
| `revenue`          | Annual revenue (in millions USD)    |
| `employees`        | Number of employees                 |
| `office_location`  | Headquarters location               |
| `subsidiary_of`    | Parent company                      |

### **2. `products`**
Details of the hardware products sold:
| Field         | Description                  |
|---------------|------------------------------|
| `product`     | Product name                |
| `series`      | Product series              |
| `sales_price` | Suggested retail price (USD) |

### **3. `sales_teams`**
Details of sales teams and agents:
| Field            | Description                |
|-------------------|----------------------------|
| `sales_agent`     | Sales agent name           |
| `manager`         | Sales manager name         |
| `regional_office` | Regional office location   |

### **4. `sales_pipeline`**
Tracks sales opportunities and deal outcomes:
| Field            | Description                                       |
|-------------------|---------------------------------------------------|
| `opportunity_id`  | Unique identifier for each opportunity            |
| `sales_agent`     | Sales agent managing the opportunity              |
| `product`         | Product involved in the deal                     |
| `account`         | Company involved in the deal                     |
| `deal_stage`      | Sales pipeline stage (Prospecting, Engaging, Won/Lost) |
| `engage_date`     | Date when the "Engaging" stage began              |
| `close_date`      | Date when the deal was closed (Won/Lost)          |
| `close_value`     | Revenue (or potential revenue) from the deal      |

## 💡 Questions Answered
Here are some of the key questions and business problems tackled in this project:

1. **Revenue Insights**:
   - What is the total revenue by sector and product series?
   - What is the total revenue generated by each sales agent?
   - Which product series has the highest revenue contribution?

2. **Deal Analysis**:
   - How many deals were won, lost, or are in the "Engaging" stage?
   - What is the average deal closure time for each sales agent?
   - What is the lost revenue for each sector and region?

3. **Sales Team Performance**:
   - Which sales agents have the highest win rates?
   - How do regional offices compare in terms of revenue and deal success rates?

4. **Customer Insights**:
   - Which accounts contribute the most revenue?
   - Which sectors are the most profitable?
